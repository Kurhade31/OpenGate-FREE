# Content policy

- Every official claim: `sourceType: official` + URL + `verifiedAt` (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Secondary sources only when official is silent; label `verified-secondary`.
- Editorial guides answer: what / why / prerequisites / learn / practice / mistakes / next.
- Community content stays in review until checked.
- Copyright: link to official papers; original questions preferred; never copy paid banks. Provenance in `data/provenance/`.
- AI may draft; humans must verify official dates, eligibility, rules, answer keys, legal claims.
- No fake stats, testimonials, rankings, or AI answers presented as authoritative.
